# Changelog

## Unreleased

## [1.0.1](https://github.com/anguria-studio/Obolo/releases/tag/v1.0.1) — 2026-11-27

- Fixed a logo lookup that could contact the wrong website when a pasted billing link contained an email address.
- Added **Help → Privacy Policy** so you can read how Obolo handles your data.
- The Mac App Store build fetches icons from subscription websites. The optional Google logo fallback is available in direct downloads.

## [1.0.0](https://github.com/anguria-studio/Obolo/releases/tag/v1.0.0) — 2026-11-27

- Track subscriptions, upcoming renewals, and price history.
- Compare spending by month, category, and subscription across currencies.
- Set payment reminders, add renewals to Apple Calendar, and use Shortcuts to view or add subscriptions.
- Import and export CSVs, back up your library, and optionally sync it between Macs with iCloud.
- The direct-download edition checks for updates automatically and asks before installing. Use Settings → Updates to turn checks off or check immediately.
